## Working summary (kickoff §3)

Pick site(s) (catalogue + custom coordinates) and a date/time →
show the Sun and the Earth relative to the **real terrain horizon** →
time to sunset / Earthset →
power & communications metrics (solar elevation, sunlight duration, Earth visibility windows) →
**compare several scenarios side by side** and explore how a date/site change alters feasibility →
timelines / animation over a lunar day →
usable by mission planners **and** the public.

Challenge examples include Apollo and other countries' landing sites (outside the polar
DEM coverage — listed as "outside coverage"); the hero region is the lunar south pole.
